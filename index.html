VendGuard

Incident management platform for vending machine fleets, with triage, SLA control, field intervention and immutable auditing.

It solves the loss of traceability and duplicated reports in maintenance: a unique database index prevents two concurrent tickets for the same machine, perishable food machines are forced to maximum priority with an SLA under 4 hours, and physical deletion is impossible because everything lands in an append-only log. Built with PHP 8.2 in clean architecture and native PDO, Vue.js 3 on the frontend, MariaDB and a complete REST API; it includes SVG QR code generation with Reed-Solomon error correction and no extensions, MTTR metrics with percentage variation, and technicians can only see their own metrics, never their colleagues'. It is also my first contact with Vue.js, and the biggest technical challenge of the project. Status: 3,792 tests across 120 suites at 100%.
